Clockwork Bolts Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Clockwork Bolts ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the levels you have cleared, the level in progress, and the Calm swing, sound and vibration settings. This is saved only on your own device and never leaves it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
