Gobble Town Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Gobble Town ("the app") is an arcade game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection: the town maps are inside the app.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your best results for each town, your sticker book (what you have swallowed, and the landmarks), a calm-mode game in progress, and your sound and vibration settings. All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Location

The towns are real neighbourhoods from OpenStreetMap, built into the app. The app does not use or ask for your location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
