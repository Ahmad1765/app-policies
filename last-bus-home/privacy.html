Last Bus Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Last Bus Home: Color Sort ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Your progress and settings (the stops you have cleared, the stop in progress, night service and sound options) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

The time of day

To switch between day and night service, the app reads the time on your phone's clock. This happens on the device only, and the time is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
