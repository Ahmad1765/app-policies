Peak Lodge Solitaire Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Peak Lodge Solitaire ("the app") is a card game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

For each player you add, the app keeps the name you typed, the deals cleared, the summits reached and the day each was reached, the longest streak, and the deal in progress. The card, sound and vibration settings are kept too. All of this is saved only on your own device and never leaves it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Player names

Names are used only to tell the players on one device apart. Use any name you like; it does not need to be a real one.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
