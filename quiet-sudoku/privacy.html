Quiet Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Quiet Sudoku: Classic & Killer ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (puzzles in progress, puzzles solved, times, daily puzzles, postcard pieces, puzzle books made, display and sound options) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Puzzle books

When you tap "Make the PDF", the app creates a PDF of sudoku puzzles, saves it in the app's own temporary storage on your phone, and hands it to the Android share menu. You choose where it goes (a printer, a message, a file). The PDF contains only puzzles and their answers. Quiet Sudoku itself sends nothing anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
