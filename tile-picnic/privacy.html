Tile Picnic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Tile Picnic: Triple Match ("the app") is a puzzle game for one player, or two taking turns on one phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Your progress and settings (levels cleared, the recipe cards you have collected, the level in progress, market days played, duel results, the season setting and sound options) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

The date

The daily market uses the date on your phone to choose that day's board and the month's produce. This happens on the device only, and the date is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
