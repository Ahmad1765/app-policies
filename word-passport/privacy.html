Word Passport Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Word Passport: Word Search ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (puzzles completed, stars, passport and daily stamps, hints, bonus words, local words found, sound options) are saved only on your own device. They never leave it unless you choose to send a postcard. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Postcards and spoken words

When you tap "Send a postcard", the app draws a postcard picture (a greeting, a fact about the city and words you found), saves it in the app's own temporary storage on your phone, and hands it to the Android share menu. You choose where it goes. Word Passport itself sends nothing anywhere.

Greetings and local words are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The app only passes it the word to say; it records nothing and uses no microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (such as ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
